## 项目简介 | Project Overview

本项目托管了一份关于**宇宙本体论**与**物理演化框架**的研究白皮书。该理论跳出了传统的“物质主义”视角，尝试从“事件”和“信息流”的角度重新解释宇宙的运行规律。

This repository hosts a research whitepaper on **Universal Ontology** and **Physical Evolution**. The theory moves beyond traditional "materialism," attempting to re-explain the laws of the universe through the lens of "Events" and "Information Flow."

## 核心主张 | Core Propositions

* **从物质到事件 (From Matter to Events):** 认为宇宙的基本构成单位不是静态的物质粒子，而是动态的事件。
    * *Proposes that the fundamental building blocks of the universe are dynamic events rather than static material particles.*
* **信息与物理的统一 (Unifying Information and Physics):** 探讨了微观核物理如何作为底层代码生成宏观现实。
    * *Explores how microscopic nuclear physics acts as the "source code" that generates macroscopic reality.*
* **文明的新度量 (New Metrics for Civilization):** 提出了一种基于结构复杂度而非能源消耗的文明进步衡量方式。
    * *Introduces a way to measure civilizational progress based on structural complexity rather than energy consumption.*

## 项目声明与哲学背景 / Project Disclaimer & Philosophical Roots

> **注意 / Note**: 本框架是一个探索性的思想实验。为了读者能更准确地理解其核心逻辑、局限性及产出背景，特说明如下。
> This framework is an exploratory thought experiment. The following clarifications are provided to help readers accurately understand its core logic, limitations, and origin.

### 1. 科学立场与方法论 / Scientific Stance & Methodology
* **对理性的尊重 (Respect for Reason)**: 本框架保持对**数学、数理逻辑及科学方法**的绝对尊重。作者无意发展任何形式的“民科”理论。
  This framework maintains absolute respect for **mathematics, mathematical logic, and the scientific method**. The author has no intention of developing "pseudo-science."
* **直觉导向 (Intuition-Led)**: 由于作者本身的数学和物理水平有限，大部分推论过程是高度**概念化**和**形而上**的。其重点在于提供**“直觉的方向”**。
  Due to the author’s limited background in advanced physics and mathematics, most inferences are highly **conceptual and metaphysical**. The emphasis is on the **"direction of intuition"** rather than rigorous formal proofs.

### 2. 生成背景与局限性 / Genesis & Limitations
* **人机共创 (Human-AI Co-creation)**: 整个框架主要是作者与 **Gemini 3** 在 2025 年 10 月至 12 月期间通过一系列深度对话构建的。
  The entire framework was primarily constructed through a series of deep dialogues between the author and **Gemini 3** from October to December 2025.
* **未经严苛审计 (Lack of Formal Audit)**: 当前内容是由 AI 根据对话上下文直接输出，未经详细的 Fact Check 和逻辑审查，错误在所难免。
  The current white paper was generated by Gemini 3 based on conversation context. It has not undergone detailed fact-checking or rigorous logical auditing; therefore, errors are inevitable.
* **术语偏差 (Terminological Nuances)**: 部分术语（如频繁出现的“**深层物理学/Deep Physics**”）实为作者给对话 Session 起的名字，而非标准学术概念，但不影响核心内容的传达。
  Certain terms (e.g., the frequently mentioned "**Deep Physics**") are actually the title given to the conversation session by the author rather than established academic concepts, though this does not affect the core message.

### 3. 思想溯源 / Philosophical Roots
* **理论基石 (Theoretical Foundations)**: 本框架的起点构筑在**斯宾诺莎（Spinoza）的一元论**思想上，并受到**惠勒（John Wheeler）单电子宇宙论**的启发。
  The framework is grounded in **Spinoza’s Monism** and inspired by **John Wheeler’s One-Electron Universe** hypothesis.
* **原创与重合 (Originality & Convergence)**: 除上述基石外，大部分想法为作者原创。不可避免地，部分观点可能与已有理论重复或相似，在此不一一列举。
  Beyond these foundations, most ideas are the author's original synthesis. Inevitably, some concepts may overlap or converge with existing theories, which are not listed exhaustively here.

### 4. 关于灵感来源的特殊说明 / Special Note on Inspiration
* **启示与视图 (Revelations & Visions)**: 作者的很多观点来自于“与神合一”状态下获得的**大量启示、直接“视图”或“提示”**。读者完全可以无视这一点，这不会影响逻辑层面的讨论。
  Many of the author’s insights stem from **revelations, direct "visions," or "prompts"** received during states of "Oneness with God." Readers may choose to disregard this entirely, as it should not affect the logical discussion of the framework.
* **信仰立场 (Stance on Faith)**: 作者是有神论者，但**排斥宗教**，并否认当前主流宗教对“神”的刻画。
  The author is a theist but **rejects organized religion** and denies the depictions of "God" found in mainstream religious doctrines.
