## 项目简介 | Project Overview

本项目托管了一份关于**宇宙本体论**与**物理演化框架**的研究白皮书。该理论跳出了传统的“物质主义”视角，尝试从“事件”和“信息流”的角度重新解释宇宙的运行规律。

This repository hosts a research whitepaper on **Universal Ontology** and **Physical Evolution**. The theory moves beyond traditional "materialism," attempting to re-explain the laws of the universe through the lens of "Events" and "Information Flow."

---

## 核心主张 | Core Propositions

* **从物质到事件 (From Matter to Events):** 认为宇宙的基本构成单位不是静态的物质粒子，而是动态的事件。
    * *Proposes that the fundamental building blocks of the universe are dynamic events rather than static material particles.*
* **信息与物理的统一 (Unifying Information and Physics):** 探讨了微观核物理如何作为底层代码生成宏观现实。
    * *Explores how microscopic nuclear physics acts as the "source code" that generates macroscopic reality.*
* **文明的新度量 (New Metrics for Civilization):** 提出了一种基于结构复杂度而非能源消耗的文明进步衡量方式。
    * *Introduces a way to measure civilizational progress based on structural complexity rather than energy consumption.*
